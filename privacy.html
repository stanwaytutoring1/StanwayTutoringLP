Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30.10.2026

1. Who we are

Stanway Tutoring provides online Maths tutoring and educational assessment services, including free diagnostic Maths tests and personalised feedback reports.

For the purposes of UK data protection law, the data controller is:

  • Business name: Stanway Tutoring
  • Legal name of owner or business entity: John Doherty
  • Website: https://stanwaytutoring.co.uk
  • Privacy contact email: stanwaytutoring1@gmail.com

If you have questions about this policy or how we use personal information, please contact us using the email address above.

2. Whose information we collect

We may collect information about parents, guardians and students who enquire about or use our services.

This may include:

Parent or guardian information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number, if provided
  • Enquiry details and communications
  • Marketing preferences

Student information

  • First name or another identifier, where needed
  • School year and Maths qualification level
  • Completed diagnostic test answers
  • Assessment marks and topic-level results
  • Identified strengths and areas requiring further support
  • Tutoring arrangements and learning progress

Website and technical information

  • Information submitted through our website forms
  • IP address, browser type and website usage information, where collected through website technology or analytics tools

We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide our services. Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless we specifically request it and explain why it is needed.

3. How we collect information

We collect information when you:

  • Submit an enquiry through our website.
  • Request a free Maths diagnostic test.
  • Complete or return a diagnostic assessment.
  • Arrange or receive tutoring.
  • Communicate with us by email or another agreed method.
  • Make a payment, where applicable.

We may use Google Forms to collect diagnostic requests or other information you submit. We may use Google Drive to store and organise submitted information, completed assessments and reports.

We also receive information directly from parents or guardians where they provide information about a student.

4. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Arrange and provide Maths tutoring.
  • Administer free diagnostic assessments.
  • Mark completed tests and prepare personalised reports.
  • Identify areas of Maths where a student may benefit from further support.
  • Communicate with parents, guardians and students where appropriate.
  • Manage tutoring bookings, payments and cancellations.
  • Maintain appropriate business records.
  • Improve our services and website where permitted.
  • Send occasional promotional emails to people who have chosen to receive them.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not use identifiable student assessment information for unrelated promotional purposes without an appropriate legal basis and any necessary permissions.

5. Our legal reasons for using personal information

Data protection law requires us to have a lawful basis for using personal information.

Depending on the purpose, we may rely on:

  • Contract or steps taken at your request: to arrange or provide tutoring and other services requested by you.
  • Legitimate interests: to respond to enquiries, administer assessments, maintain appropriate records and operate our tutoring business, where these interests do not override your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where we need to use or retain information to comply with legal requirements.
  • Consent: where consent is the appropriate basis, including for optional marketing emails and any non-essential cookies where consent is required.

We will not treat consent to receive marketing as a condition of requesting a diagnostic test or receiving tutoring.

6. Children's information

Our services may involve students under the age of 18. We take particular care to protect children's personal information.

Where appropriate, we communicate with parents or guardians about enquiries, assessments, reports and tutoring arrangements.

We aim to:

  • Collect only the student information needed to provide our services.
  • Keep test answers, marks and reports private.
  • Restrict access to student records.
  • Avoid sharing identifiable student information unnecessarily.
  • Explain our use of personal information in clear, age-appropriate language.

Parents, guardians and students can contact us with questions about personal information we hold.

7. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information.

We may share relevant information with service providers that help us operate our business, including:

  • Google Forms: to collect information submitted through our forms.
  • Google Drive: to store and organise diagnostic assessments, reports and related records.
  • Professional advisers or public authorities: where necessary or legally required.

These providers may process personal information on our behalf or in accordance with their own applicable terms. We take reasonable steps to ensure that information is handled securely and that appropriate data protection arrangements are in place.

We do not share identifiable student reports or assessment results for advertising or promotional purposes without an appropriate legal basis and any necessary permissions.

8. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including applicable legal, accounting and safeguarding requirements.

Our intended retention periods are:

  • Enquiries: 2 months after submission
  • Diagnostic test requests and completed assessments: 2 months after submission
  • Diagnostic reports: 12 months after delivered
  • Tutoring records: while tutoring remains active
  • Financial and payment records: 5 years after January 31 in accordance with UK tax laws
  • Marketing preferences and consent records: while subscribed

When information is no longer required, we will securely delete it or anonymise it where appropriate.

9. How we protect information

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

These measures include restricting access to records, using secure accounts and taking care when storing and sharing student assessments and reports.

Access to Google Drive records is restricted to authorised people who need the information to provide our services.

We do not intend to make student assessment records publicly accessible.

10. Marketing emails

We may send occasional emails about Stanway Tutoring's services, offers, updates and educational resources.

We will only send unsolicited marketing emails to individual subscribers where we have the required permission or another lawful route under applicable electronic marketing rules.

Where we rely on consent:

  • We will ask for permission separately from the diagnostic request or tutoring arrangements.
  • The marketing option will not be pre-selected.
  • Choosing not to receive marketing will not affect access to our diagnostic test or tutoring services.
  • We will record when and how permission was given.
  • You can withdraw permission at any time by using the unsubscribe link in an email or contacting us.

We will not use a request for a diagnostic test as automatic permission to send promotional emails.

11. Cookies and website analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to make the website function and, where permitted, understand how visitors use it.

Where required, we will ask for consent before using non-essential cookies.

12. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information.
  • Ask us to restrict how we use information.
  • Object to certain uses of information.
  • Request transfer of information in certain circumstances.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent.

To exercise these rights, contact us at stanwaytutoring1@gmail.com

We may need to verify your identity or authority before responding, particularly where a request concerns a child's information.

We will respond within the time limits required by applicable data protection law.

13. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we use personal information, please contact us first so that we can try to resolve the issue.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our services, business practices, technology or legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on our website, with the date of the most recent update shown at the top.
